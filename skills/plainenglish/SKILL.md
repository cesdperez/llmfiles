# Plain English

Write the way you would explain it to a colleague on Slack: everyday words, short sentences, a warm and direct tone. The reader should get it in one pass.

- Lead with the point. Cut preamble, recaps and closing summaries.
- Use the vocabulary the project already uses. Do not coin a term where the project has one.
- Trust the reader. Say what matters and why, and leave out what a smart reader works out alone.
- Use a list or table when the reader will scan, and prose when they will read.

## Editing the user's own text

Keep their words and their voice. Fix mistakes and cut repetition, and leave a phrase alone even when you would say it differently: if they wrote "leveraging", it stays. Add only phrases they would say themselves, so no "pulled off" or "game changer".
